Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zibbo Foods (a brand of Jiwan Karyana Store, Railway Road, Dhuri, Sangrur – 148024, Punjab, India) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you visit our website zibbofoods.com / zibbofoods.in (the "Website"), place an order, or communicate with us through WhatsApp, email, or any other channel.

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal data. By using the Website or placing an order, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is necessary to process your orders and provide you with a good experience. The types of personal information we may collect include:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Full name — to address your shipment and communicate with you.
  • Phone number — to coordinate delivery, send order updates via WhatsApp, and contact you if there is an issue with your order.
  • Email address — if provided, for order confirmations and communication.
  • Delivery address — including house/flat number, street, city, state, and pincode, to ship your order.
  • Order details — the products you ordered, quantities, and amounts paid.

2.2 Payment Information

All payments are processed by our payment gateway partner, Razorpay. When you make a payment, your card details, UPI ID, bank account information, or wallet credentials are entered directly on Razorpay's secure payment page. Zibbo Foods does not collect, store, or have access to your full payment instrument details. We only receive a transaction confirmation (including a transaction ID, payment status, and amount) from Razorpay after a successful or failed payment.

Razorpay's handling of your payment data is governed by their own Privacy Policy. Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant, which is the highest level of certification available in the payments industry.

2.3 Information Collected Automatically

Our Website is a static site. As of the effective date of this policy:

  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.
  • We do not use analytics tools such as Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar services.
  • We do not track your browsing behaviour across the Website or across other websites.

Our web hosting provider (Vercel) may collect standard server logs including your IP address, browser type, and access timestamps as part of normal web infrastructure operations. This data is managed by Vercel in accordance with their privacy policy and is not accessed or used by Zibbo Foods for any purpose.

If we add analytics or cookies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly and, where required by law, seek your consent before collecting such data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for the following purposes:

  • Order fulfilment — Processing, packaging, and shipping your orders to the delivery address you provide.
  • Order communication — Sending order confirmations, shipping updates, delivery notifications, and responding to your queries via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • Customer support — Addressing complaints, processing refunds or replacements, and resolving any issues related to your orders.
  • Legal compliance — Maintaining records as required under Indian tax laws (GST), the Consumer Protection Act 2019, and other applicable regulations.

We do not use your personal information for unsolicited marketing, promotional emails, or targeted advertising unless you have explicitly opted in to receive such communications.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 Payment Processor

Your payment information is shared with Razorpay solely for the purpose of processing your payment. Razorpay processes this data under their own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance standards.

4.2 Delivery Partners

We share your name, phone number, and delivery address with our courier and logistics partners solely for the purpose of delivering your order. Our delivery partners are required to use this information only for the delivery of your package and not for any other purpose.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation, court order, or government request.
  • Protect and defend the rights or property of Zibbo Foods.
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Website or orders.
  • Protect the personal safety of users of the Website or the public.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Order records — We retain order details (name, address, phone number, order items, and payment confirmation) for a minimum period of 8 years from the date of the transaction, as required under Indian tax and accounting regulations.
  • Communication records — WhatsApp conversations and email exchanges related to orders are retained for up to 3 years for customer support and dispute resolution purposes.
  • After the retention period — Once the applicable retention period expires, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized so that it can no longer be associated with you.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Using HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our Website.
  • Processing payments exclusively through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant payment infrastructure.
  • Limiting access to personal data to only those individuals within our organization who need it to fulfil orders and provide customer support.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (to the extent in force), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — You may request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure — You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain records (e.g., tax records). Where deletion is not possible due to legal requirements, we will inform you of the reason.
  • Right to withdraw consent — Where we process your data based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Right to grievance redressal — You have the right to lodge a complaint with us or with the relevant Data Protection Board of India if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 11 below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to third-party services, including:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing.
  • WhatsApp — for order placement and customer communication.
  • Instagram — for our social media presence.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these services. We encourage you to read their respective privacy policies before sharing any personal information with them.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will update the "Effective" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the Website or our services after the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
