Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective: October 2026

At Zibbo Foods, we are committed to delivering your premium makhana fresh and on time. This Shipping & Delivery Policy outlines how we handle order processing, shipping charges, delivery timelines, and related matters for all orders placed through our website (zibbofoods.com) or via WhatsApp.

1. Shipping Coverage

1.1 Domestic Shipping

We currently ship to all serviceable pin codes across India, including metro cities, tier-2 and tier-3 cities, towns, and rural areas. Estimated delivery varies from 3-5 to 5-7 days based on shipment partners and pincode (see Section 3).

1.2 International Shipping

At this time, Zibbo Foods does not offer international shipping. We only ship within India. We hope to expand internationally in the future and will update this policy accordingly.

2. Shipping Charges

  • Orders above ₹500: Free shipping across India.
  • Orders below ₹500: A flat shipping fee of ₹50 is applied at checkout.

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be clearly displayed before you confirm your order. There are no hidden fees.

3. Estimated Delivery Timelines

Estimated delivery can vary from 3-5 days to 5-7 days based on shipment partners and pincode. Delivery times are estimated from the date of dispatch (not the date of order placement).

Destination Estimated Delivery
Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad) 3-5 business days
Rest of India (tier-2, tier-3 cities, towns, and all other areas) 5-7 business days

Note: These are estimated timelines. Actual delivery may be faster or slower depending on factors beyond our control, including courier operations, weather conditions, public holidays, and local disruptions.

4. Order Processing

4.1 Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. Business days are Monday through Saturday, excluding Indian public holidays.

4.2 Order Confirmation

Once your order is placed and payment is confirmed, you will receive an order confirmation via WhatsApp or email with your order details and estimated delivery date.

4.3 Order Dispatch

After processing, your order will be handed over to our courier partner. You will be notified via WhatsApp once your order is dispatched.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, we will share the tracking details (tracking number and courier partner name) via WhatsApp to the number you provided at checkout. You can use these details to track your shipment on the courier partner's website or app.

If you do not receive tracking information within 3 business days of placing your order, please contact us:

  • WhatsApp: +91 94642 68002
  • Email: zibbofoods@gmail.com

6. Delivery Partners

Zibbo Foods uses third-party courier partners to deliver your orders. While we work with reliable logistics providers, please note:

  • Zibbo Foods is not responsible for delays caused by courier partners, including but not limited to operational delays, incorrect routing, or failed delivery attempts.
  • Zibbo Foods is not liable for delays caused by force majeure events such as natural disasters, floods, strikes, pandemics, government-imposed restrictions, or severe weather conditions.
  • If your shipment is significantly delayed (more than 5 days beyond the estimated delivery date), please contact us and we will coordinate with the courier partner on your behalf.

7. Delivery Attempts and Undelivered Orders

7.1 Delivery Attempts

Courier partners typically make 2-3 delivery attempts. If you are unavailable at the delivery address, the courier may attempt redelivery the next business day. Please ensure someone is available to receive the package.

7.2 Undelivered or Returned Shipments

If the package cannot be delivered after all attempts (due to incorrect address, recipient unavailable, address inaccessible, etc.), it will be returned to us. In such cases:

  • We will contact you on WhatsApp to confirm or update the delivery address.
  • Re-shipment may attract additional shipping charges.
  • If you do not respond within 7 days, a refund will be processed after deducting the applicable shipping costs.

7.3 Incorrect Address

Please double-check your delivery address before confirming your order. Zibbo Foods is not responsible for non-delivery or delayed delivery due to an incorrect or incomplete address provided by the customer.

8. Packaging

All Zibbo Foods products are carefully packed to ensure they reach you in perfect condition. Our makhana packets are placed in protective outer packaging to prevent damage during transit. If your order arrives with damaged packaging, please refer to our Refund & Cancellation Policy for next steps.

9. Bulk and Wholesale Orders

For bulk or wholesale orders, shipping timelines and charges may differ based on order quantity and delivery location. Please contact us on WhatsApp at +91 94642 68002 before placing a bulk order so we can provide accurate shipping estimates and arrange the best logistics option for your needs.
