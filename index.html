Frequently Asked Questions

What is makhana?

Makhana (also called fox nuts, lotus seeds, or phool makhana) is an edible seed harvested from ponds across Bihar, India. It is one of the healthiest snacks available — naturally rich in protein, fibre, and calcium, with very low fat. It has been a staple in Indian households for centuries, especially during fasting.
