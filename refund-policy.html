Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: October 2026

At Zibbo Foods, we want you to be completely satisfied with every order. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions under which you may cancel an order, request a return, or receive a refund for products purchased through our website (zibbofoods.com) or via WhatsApp ordering.

1. Order Cancellation

1.1 Cancellation Window

You may cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it, provided the order has not already been dispatched. To cancel, contact us immediately via:

  • WhatsApp: +91 94642 68002
  • Email: zibbofoods@gmail.com

1.2 After Dispatch

Once your order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. You may, however, refuse the delivery at the time it arrives. Refused shipments will be treated as returns and processed in accordance with Section 2 below.

1.3 Cancellation by Zibbo Foods

We reserve the right to cancel an order in the following circumstances:

  • Product is out of stock or discontinued
  • Pricing or product information error on the website
  • Suspected fraudulent or duplicate order
  • Delivery address is unserviceable by our courier partners

If we cancel your order, you will receive a full refund to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

2. Returns & Exchanges

2.1 Return & Exchange Window

We accept returns and exchanges within 7 days of delivery, but only in cases of genuine issues or concerns with the delivered items. Returns or exchanges for change of mind are not accepted.

2.2 Eligible Reasons for Return or Exchange

Returns and exchanges are accepted only in the following cases:

  • Damaged or defective product: The packet arrived torn, crushed, or with a broken seal due to transit damage.
  • Wrong item delivered: You received a product different from what you ordered.
  • Quality concern: The product has a genuine quality issue that makes it unfit for consumption.

2.3 Non-Returnable Items

We cannot accept returns in the following cases:

  • Change of mind — returns are not accepted simply because you changed your mind
  • Products that have been partially or fully consumed without a genuine quality complaint
  • Products returned after the 7-day return window

2.4 How to Initiate a Return or Exchange

To start a return or exchange, follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 94642 68002 or email zibbofoods@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery.
  • Step 2: Share your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photographs of the damaged or defective items and packaging within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Step 3: Our team will review your request and respond within 24-48 hours with return or exchange instructions.
  • Step 4: Ship the product back as instructed, or our team will arrange a reverse pickup where available.

3. Reporting Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, you must share photos of the damaged or defective items within 48 hours of delivery. To report:

  • Send clear photos of the damaged product, packaging, and shipping label on WhatsApp to +91 94642 68002.
  • Alternatively, email the photos and your order details to zibbofoods@gmail.com.

We will assess the damage and, if confirmed, either ship a replacement at no additional cost or process a full refund. Reporting damage after 48 hours may limit our ability to process your claim.

4. Refund Process

4.1 Refund Timeline

Once your return is received at our facility and inspected, we will process your refund within 5-7 business days. The refund will be issued to your original payment method:

  • UPI / Net Banking: Refund credited within 5-7 business days.
  • Credit/Debit Card: Refund credited within 5-7 business days; may take an additional 2-3 days to reflect in your bank statement depending on your card issuer.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) orders: Refund issued via bank transfer. You will be asked to provide your bank account details (account number, IFSC code) on WhatsApp or email.

4.2 Partial Refunds

In some cases, a partial refund may be issued:

  • If only some items in a multi-item order are returned, the refund will cover only the returned items.
  • If the returned product shows signs of use, tampering, or is not in its original condition, a partial refund or no refund may be offered at our discretion.

4.3 No Refund Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Change of mind on opened/unsealed products
  • Products returned without prior approval from our team
  • Products returned after the 7-day return window
  • Damage caused by the customer after delivery

5. Shipping Costs for Returns

  • If the issue is our fault (damaged product, wrong item delivered, defective packaging): Zibbo Foods will bear the full cost of return shipping.
  • If the return is for any other reason (e.g., change of mind on an unopened product): The customer is responsible for the return shipping cost.

6. Replacements

For damaged or defective products, we may offer a replacement instead of a refund, subject to product availability. Replacements will be shipped within 2-3 business days after the return is received and inspected. If the product is out of stock, a full refund will be processed instead.

7. Wholesale and Bulk Orders

Refund and return policies for wholesale or bulk orders may differ. Please contact us on WhatsApp at +91 94642 68002 to discuss terms before placing a bulk order.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Refund & Cancellation Policy, or need help with a return or refund, please reach out to us:

  • WhatsApp: +91 94642 68002
  • Email: zibbofoods@gmail.com
  • Business Address: Railway Road, M/s Jiwan Karyana Store, Dhuri, Dhuri, Sangrur, Punjab – 148024

We aim to respond to all queries within 24-48 hours.
